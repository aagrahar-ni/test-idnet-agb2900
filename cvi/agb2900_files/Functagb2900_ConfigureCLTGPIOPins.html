agb2900_ConfigureCLTGPIOPins

ViStatus agb2900_ConfigureCLTGPIOPins (ViSession instrumentHandle, ViInt32 channel, ViBoolean _VI_FAR pinsForResultOutput[], ViInt32 pinForBUSY, ViInt32 pinForSOT, ViInt32 pinForEOT, ViInt32 arraySize);

Purpose

Assigns the GPIO pins used for the result output, or  assigns the GPIO pin for the BUSY (busy), EOT (end of test), or SOT (start of test) signal line for the composite limit test.
Note:
This function is not applicable for B296x family models B2961A/B2962A, B2961B/B2962B, and B2961C/B2962C.
